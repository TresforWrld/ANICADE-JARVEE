ANICADE VISION Voice Guide

Speak naturally to command Anicade (ensure the microphone Orb is active):

Reading & Analysis

  • "Read screen" - Runs vision/OCR and speaks the detected text.
  • "Explain screen" - Explains what is currently shown.
  • "Scan this document" - Analyzes text from camera capture.

Task Automation

  • "Help me with this school question" - Translates and explains equations/slides.
  • "Suggest a reply" - Scans visible message layout and suggests replies.
  • "Generate an ad for [product]" - Generates a visual advertisement banner.

Utility Dictation

  • "Start voice typing" - Enters notepad mode where spoken words are typed directly.
  • "Stop typing" - Returns to general screen reading/chatbot mode.
  • "Clear notebook" - Erases notepad context.

Creative Ad Banner Generation

ANICADE VISION includes a free Image Ad Generator connected to Pollinations.ai. You can create advertising campaigns dynamically.

How to generate ads?

1. Share a document or product page on screen (or upload an image).
2. Click the "Image Ad Generator" tool, or speak: "generate ad for [product/theme]".
3. ANICADE VISION reads keywords on screen, drafts a prompt, and displays a marketing image banner instantly.
4. Click "Save Ad Banner" to download the image file to your device.

Immersive Standalone PWA setup

Launch ANICADE VISION directly from your desktop or phone homescreen:

Desktop PCs

  • Tap the animated "Install App" button next to status in top header.
  • Confirm the browser installation prompt.
  • Launches full-screen with no browser address bars for clean HUD aesthetic.

Mobile Devices

  • Tap Install App at the top bar.
  • On iOS, tap Safari's Share icon and select Add to Home Screen.
  • This loads service workers in the background, permitting offline usage.
