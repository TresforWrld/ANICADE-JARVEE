Jarvis Voice AI: Tactical Screen Suite

Local OCR engines, free visual generators, and zero-setup efficiency.

Welcome to the updated **JARVIS VISION** dashboard, engineered to operate fully offline and client-side without API keys. Utilizing advanced client-side text extraction engines (**Tesseract.js**), Jarvis scans images, camera feeds, or PC desktops, reading coordinates and executing commands. Integrated with high-fidelity British voice synthesis, Jarvis responds with Tony Stark's personal assistant demeanor.

1
Capture Input: Activate screen sharing, link mobile cameras, or drop screenshot pictures.
2
Microphone Listen: Voice triggers are parsed continuously. Say "Jarvis, read screen" or click the orb.
3
Local OCR Scan: Tesseract.js extracts real text coordinates locally on the fly.
4
Jarvis Response: Jarvis explains academic homework, drafts WhatsApp text, or spawns image ads.

Jarvis Voice Action Directory

Say these phrases to command Jarvis (ensure the microphone Orb is active):

Reading & Analysis

  • "Jarvis, read screen" - Runs local OCR and speaks the text detected.
  • "Jarvis, explain screen" - Reads page and gives witty explanations.
  • "Jarvis, scan document" - Analyzes text from camera capture.

Task Automation

  • "Jarvis, school help" - Translates algebra formulas on screen and explains step-by-step.
  • "Jarvis, auto reply" - Scans visible message layout and suggests replies.
  • "Jarvis, generate ad for [product]" - Generates a visual advertisement banner.

Utility Dictation

  • "Jarvis, voice typing" - Enters notepad mode. Speaks are typed directly to notebooks.
  • "stop typing" - Returns Jarvis to general screen reading command mode.
  • "Jarvis, clear notebook" - Erases notepad context.

Creative Ad Banner Generation

JARVIS VISION includes a free Image Ad Generator connected to Pollinations.ai. You can create advertising campaigns dynamically.

How to generate ads?

1. Share a document or product page on screen (or upload an image).
2. Click the "Image Ad Generator" tool, or speak: "Jarvis, generate ad for [product/theme]".
3. Jarvis reads keywords on screen, drafts a prompt, and displays a marketing image banner instantly.
4. Click "Save Ad Banner" to download the image file to your device.

Immersive Standalone PWA setup

Launch JARVIS VISION directly from your desktop or phone homescreen:

Desktop PCs

  • Tap the animated "Install App" button next to status in top header.
  • Confirm the browser installation prompt.
  • Launches full-screen with no browser address bars for clean HUD aesthetic.

Mobile Devices

  • Tap Install App at the top bar.
  • On iOS, tap Safari's Share icon and select Add to Home Screen.
  • This loads service workers in the background, permitting offline usage.
